States

Idle
The notch rests as a small pill with an amber light. Hudhud breathes and blinks, and now and then glances up at the notch, stretches a wing, hops, or turns round to scratch its back. Hover the notch to peek.
Listening
The notch pours out with the app icon's eye; its rings ripple while your words appear. Hudhud opens its crest.
Thinking
A one-line plan with a soft shimmer. Hudhud looks up and nods.
Doing
Steps tick off one by one, so you see everything Mubsir touches. Right Ctrl stops it.
Pointing
The pointer flies out of the notch to the exact button and names it.
Speaking
Captions follow the voice word by word, then the Arabic line. Hudhud's beak moves with it.
Reading
Book, page and progress in the notch; the current line glows on the page.
Payment
The real amount and site. Only four Ctrl presses approve it, each one felt in the notch. Esc cancels.

Springs instead of fixed durations, set the way Apple sets them: response is how fast a value gets there, damping 1.0 means no bounce. Every value starts from where it is on screen, so nothing jumps when a state changes mid-motion. Text enters with a 10 px blur that clears as it settles.

The notch is drawn by Mubsir itself, so it looks and works the same on any PC screen, desktop or laptop, with or without a camera notch. This page is a prototype for direction only: the real notch and Hudhud are a native Windows overlay (WPF) that never takes focus, lets clicks pass through except on its own buttons, follows Windows' reduce-motion setting and reuses the springs above.
