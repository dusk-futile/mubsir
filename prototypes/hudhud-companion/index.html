Hudhud, Mubsir's companion

A working sketch of normal mode. Hudhud lives in the bottom-right corner, above the taskbar, and opens a small card only when it has something to show. Blind mode shows none of this: it is voice and sounds only.

Tip: in the Payment state press Ctrl four times quickly, or tap the button, to approve. Click Hudhud to start listening.

Listening

Crest opens, a ring pulses, live waveform and the words as they are heard.

Thinking

A soft shimmer and a one-line plan. Short, so it never feels slow.

Doing

Steps tick off one by one, so the user can see what Mubsir touches.

Pointing

A crest-orange pointer flies from Hudhud to the exact button and names it.

Speaking

Beak moves with the voice; captions in English or Arabic highlight word by word.

Reading

Book, page and progress; the current line glows on the page itself.

Payment

Real facts from the page, then four quick Ctrl presses. Nothing else can approve it.

Idle

Just Hudhud breathing and blinking. In the app it fades to a small dot after a while.

Prototype for direction only. The real companion is a native Windows window (WPF) that never steals focus, is click-through except its buttons, follows Windows "reduce motion", and uses no CPU when nothing moves. Colours, sizes and timings from this page become its motion tokens.
