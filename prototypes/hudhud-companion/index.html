Hudhud and the notch · normal mode

The notch at the top shows what Mubsir is doing. Hudhud in the corner shows how it feels about it, and naps when you don't need it. Everything moves on springs, so any state can interrupt any other without a jump. Press Play, or pick a state.

— fps

Click Hudhud to open Mubsir, or the notch to talk. Ctrl stops what Mubsir is doing and undoes it; in Payment press Ctrl four times to approve; Esc cancels. Leave Hudhud alone and it falls asleep; Ctrl or a click wakes it.

    States

    Idle
    The notch rests as a small pill with an amber light. Hudhud breathes and blinks, and now and then glances up, shakes its feathers, stretches a wing or hops. Hover the notch to peek; click Hudhud to open Mubsir.
    Listening
    The notch pours out with a fluid voice wave, and light flows in along its edge as you speak; your words appear as they are heard. Hudhud opens its crest.
    Thinking
    One small breathing light, the word Thinking, and one calm spark travelling along the edge. Hudhud looks up and nods.
    Doing
    Each step spins while it runs and is ticked only when it is finished, and a line along the edge grows with them. Ctrl stops it and undoes the finished steps in reverse, and the line shrinks back.
    Pointing
    The pointer flies out of the notch to the exact button and names it. Ctrl calls it back.
    Speaking
    Captions follow the voice word by word, then the Arabic line; the wave moves with the voice, and light flows out along the edge.
    Reading
    Book, page and progress, and what you can say next; the current line glows on the page.
    Payment
    The real amount and site. Only four Ctrl presses approve it, and each one lights a quarter of the edge. Esc cancels.
    Sleeping
    After a while with nothing to do, Hudhud tucks its head, closes its eyes and breathes slowly. Ctrl or a click wakes it, and it shakes itself awake.

    Springs instead of fixed durations, set the way Apple sets them: response is how fast a value gets there, damping 1.0 means no bounce. Every value starts from where it is on screen, so nothing jumps when a state changes mid-motion. Hudhud is a cutout puppet: ChatGPT drew its parts, and each part turns around its own joint.

    The notch is drawn by Mubsir itself, so it looks and works the same on any PC screen, desktop or laptop, with or without a camera notch. This page is a prototype for direction only: the real notch and Hudhud are a native Windows overlay (WPF) that never takes focus, lets clicks pass through except on its own buttons, follows Windows' reduce-motion setting and reuses the springs above.
