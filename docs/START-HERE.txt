Mubsir: your whole PC, by voice

Mubsir is a free voice agent for blind and low-vision people on Windows.
Hold Right Ctrl, say what you want in Arabic, English or both, and let go.
It reads your screen, works your apps, reads your books and answers out loud.

Install (about 10 minutes)

1. Extract this zip to a folder you keep, for example Documents\Mubsir.
2. Right-click setup.ps1 and choose "Run with PowerShell" as administrator.
   It installs NVDA if you don't have it, the Mubsir add-on for NVDA,
   Arabic speech and text recognition, and starts Mubsir when you sign in.
3. Mubsir opens its setup window and talks you through it.
   You need one free key from Google Gemini. The window opens the page for you.
   Copy the key and Mubsir picks it up, tests it and tells you the result.
   Keys are stored encrypted for your Windows account only.

Optional keys
- Fish Audio: a natural Arabic voice. Without it, the free Windows voice speaks Arabic.
- Claude: a paid brain (Claude Haiku), used instead of Gemini when you add it.

Keys
- Hold Right Ctrl: talk. Let go when you're done.
- Ctrl: stop speaking.
- Right Ctrl or Esc during a task: stop it.
- Win + Shift + Esc: stop everything.
- Double-tap Right Ctrl, then F1: help.

Needs 64-bit Windows 10 (22H2) or 11, a microphone and the internet.
Source, help and updates: https://github.com/dusk-futile/mubsir
